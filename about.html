About this site

Free Classic Movies is a non-commercial personal website that collects classic films in the public domain for everyone to watch for free.

The site currently features 35 films, including Charlie Chaplin comedies from the silent era, early horror films, film noir, and Chinese classics from 1929 to 1962 — such as The Goddess and Love and Duty starring Ruan Lingyu, Jackie Chan's first film Big and Little Wong Tin Bar, and Children of Troubled Times, whose theme song became the March of the Volunteers.

All films come from the Internet Archive (archive.org) and are in the public domain; videos stream directly from the Internet Archive, not through this site's servers. Poster images are also from the Internet Archive.

This site is maintained by one person in their spare time, and the film list is updated from time to time. If you find a film that won't play, or have a public-domain film to recommend, please let us know via the contact page.
