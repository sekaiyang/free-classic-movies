Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Information we collect

This is a purely static website. There are no accounts and no login, and we do not collect or store personal information such as your name, email address or home address. You don't need to provide any personal information to watch films.

Third-party services

The films and poster images on this site are hosted by the Internet Archive (archive.org). When you play a film, your browser connects directly to the Internet Archive's servers, and the Internet Archive's privacy policy applies. The site's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; when you visit, GitHub may record ordinary server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the service running.

Advertising and cookies (future plans)

This site currently shows no ads and uses no advertising code or tracking cookies. In the future, the site may use a third-party advertising service (such as Google AdSense) to help keep it running. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, count impressions, and show personalised ads based on your browsing. If that happens, you will be able to clear or block cookies in your browser settings and opt out of personalised ads through the advertising provider's own pages. This policy will be updated before any advertising service is added.

Children

The content of this site is public-domain classic films for a general audience; we do not collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, it will be updated on this page with a new date. Continuing to use this site means you accept the updated policy.

If you have any questions, please contact us via the contact page.
