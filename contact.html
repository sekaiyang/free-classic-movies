Contact us

If you find a film that won't play, have a public-domain film to recommend, or have any suggestions for this site, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact via GitHub

The easiest way is to open an Issue (a question or suggestion) in this site's GitHub repository:
github.com/sekaiyang/free-classic-movies/issues

When you submit, please briefly say which film it is and what problem you ran into — we'll look into it as soon as we see it.

Other notes

This site is maintained by one person in their spare time, so replies may not be instant — thank you for your understanding. This site offers no paid services and collects no personal information.
